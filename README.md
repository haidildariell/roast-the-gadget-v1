## The line

> My fridge beeps like mad if I hold the door open for ten seconds, but said nothing for three weeks while the milk quietly turned into cheese.

The gadget is the fridge: it screams an alarm the moment the door is held open a few seconds too long, yet never says a word while the milk inside slowly expires. Priorities.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) assembles the line from its fragments and writes it, UTF-8, to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 141 characters, well inside the 1–200 character gate.
